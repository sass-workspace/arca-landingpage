Brand guidelines & CI sheet

The rules the Arca site is built on. Anything added later — a page, a deck, a post — should be reducible to what is on this sheet. If a decision is not covered here, choose the quieter option.

01 · Colour

Two colours carry the brand.

Arca Blue

#08177E

Primary. Full-bleed anchor bands, all body type on cream.

Arca Cream

#FFFDF3

Page base, and every piece of type sitting on blue. Never pure white.

Card Cream

#FCFBF4

Card base only, half a step below the page so cards read as objects.

TODO Yellow

#F7C948

Working colour for unconfirmed copy. Never ships to production.

Blue tints for type on cream: rgba(8,23,126,.82) body · .68 secondary · .7 small labels/numerals · .2 rules. Small text (≤15px) never below .68 — WCAG AA.

Cream tints on blue: .82 body · .65–.68 captions/eyebrows · .22 rules. Small text never below .65.

Rhythm rule: cream sections are the default and blue bands are the punctuation — hero, proof, contact/footer. Never two blue bands adjacent. No third brand colour, no gradients as decoration outside the card bloom below.

02 · Typography

One serif, one sans. No exceptions.

Display — Cormorant Garamond

We build fashion brands ready for international growth.

Weights 500 (headlines) and 600 (card titles). Italic 400/500 for pull-quotes only. Line-height 1.06–1.25, letter-spacing −.01em. Headlines are balanced with deliberate breaks, never centred.

Text & UI — DM Sans

Body copy, buttons, form fields, captions and eyebrows. Weights 400, 500, 600. Body 15–17px at line-height 1.65–1.75; never below 13px.

Eyebrow / label — 12px, .22em tracking, uppercase

Mono (system) only for TODO markers and placeholder boxes.

H1 clamp(40px,4.6vw,68px) · H2 clamp(32px,3.6vw,54px) · H3 23–29px · body 15–17px · caption 12–13.5px. Five sizes, no more.

03 · Logo

The lowercase wordmark, and nothing else.

Use the cream wordmark on Arca Blue. The asset is glyph-tight and transparent, so these are letter heights, not box heights: site header 15px (13px mobile), site footer 18px, document header 24px, showcase 30px.

Clear space of at least the height of the "a" on every side.

Never place it in a circle or any container shape, recolour it, outline it, stretch it, add a tagline lock-up, or set it in a different typeface.

04 · Components

A small kit, reused.

Step 1

The grain card

Cream base, one blue bloom anchored off the top-right corner with a long falloff, grain tile at 55% multiply, 14px radius, no shadow, no border. Text always sits on the near-cream two thirds.

Buttons — one style only

Book an introduction call See our work →

Pill CTA (cream on blue bands, blue on cream) used a maximum of three times per page; the underlined text link is the only secondary action.

Logo walls

Monochrome only — cream on blue, blue on cream, via filter. Marquee with an 8%/92% edge mask, 80px gaps, 32–42s loops. Sizes are set per mark by optical mass, not uniform height: wide wordmarks 20–26px, compact or stacked marks 34–40px. Trim every file's padding and knock out its background before it goes in.

05 · Layout, motion & imagery

Grid. 1080px content column, 28px page gutter (22px mobile). Sections breathe at 130–150px vertical padding, 92px on mobile. Asymmetric splits (7/5, 5/7) over equal halves; left-aligned, never centred.

Rules. Sections open on a 1px rgba(8,23,126,.2) hairline with an eyebrow or headline directly beneath.

Radius. 14px cards, 18px header bar, 999px pills. No drop shadows anywhere except the sticky header.

Mobile. Everything stacks to one column; the four-step section becomes a horizontal snap rail; hero video becomes a full-width block; nav collapses to wordmark + CTA.

Motion. Scroll reveal only: fade up 20px, ~400ms ease-out, once. Marquees run linear and never pause. Connector lines draw in sequence. Section snap on desktop, off on mobile. Nothing parallaxes, bounces, or loops in view.

Video. Muted, playsinline, object-fit cover. Hero loops; the collection animation plays once when it reaches the vertical centre of the viewport and does not replay.

Imagery. One image per section, full-bleed or column-filling — no collages, no stock look, no device mockups. Portraits run greyscale with a slight contrast lift. Warm sunlit photography against blue and cream.

Copy. Plain, specific, first-person where it is Honor speaking. No "elevate", "unlock", "seamless", "transform". No exclamation marks, no emoji, no invented numbers — anything unconfirmed ships as a TODO marker.

06 · Before you ship

No pure #FFFFFF sits on Arca Blue.

Two type families, five sizes, one button style.

Every logo is monochrome, trimmed, and optically sized.

Blue bands are punctuation, never adjacent.

Nothing is centred that could be left-aligned.

Every unconfirmed fact is a visible TODO, not a guess.

Checked at 390px, 768px and 1440px — nothing squeezed.

Squint test: it could not be mistaken for a SaaS template.
